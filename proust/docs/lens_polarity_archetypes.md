# Lens Polarity Archetypes

This document defines a small shorthand rubric for interpreting the sign pattern across the three scoring lenses:

- `advantage`
- `prestige`
- `inclusion`

The goal is not to replace the numeric analysis.

The goal is to provide a compact interpretive mnemonic for recurring sign combinations, something closer to an archetype label than a final diagnosis.

These labels should be treated as:

- shorthand
- aggregate-level interpretive templates
- useful for orientation and comparison

They should not be treated as:

- exact psychological descriptions
- substitutes for chapter context
- claims that all units behave the same way

## Lens Reminders

- `advantage`: immediate gain or loss in the social and emotional balance of the passage
- `prestige`: gain or loss in visible rank, distinction, authority, or standing
- `inclusion`: gain or loss in belonging, acceptance, or incorporation

## The Eight Archetypes

### `advantage + / prestige + / inclusion +`

Label:

- `Ascendant`

Interpretive shorthand:

- doing well on all fronts
- immediate advantage, rank, and belonging all reinforce each other

### `advantage + / prestige + / inclusion -`

Label:

- `Glamorous`

Interpretive shorthand:

- powerful or impressive, but not embraced
- rises and wins in ways that cost acceptance or intimacy

### `advantage + / prestige - / inclusion +`

Label:

- `Beloved`

Interpretive shorthand:

- warmly held and locally effective without corresponding public distinction

### `advantage + / prestige - / inclusion -`

Label:

- `Defiant`

Interpretive shorthand:

- capable of advantage wins from a socially marginal or unincorporated position

### `advantage - / prestige + / inclusion +`

Label:

- `Enduring`

Interpretive shorthand:

- retains standing and belonging despite repeated advantage setbacks

### `advantage - / prestige + / inclusion -`

Label:

- `Estranged`

Interpretive shorthand:

- recognized, elevated, or distinguished, but locally damaged and cut off from belonging

### `advantage - / prestige - / inclusion +`

Label:

- `Cherished`

Interpretive shorthand:

- loved or accepted without corresponding rank or immediate advantage

### `advantage - / prestige - / inclusion -`

Label:

- `Fallen`

Interpretive shorthand:

- broadly losing across immediate outcome, rank, and belonging

## Use Rule

The safest use of this rubric is:

1. determine the aggregate sign in each lens
2. map the sign pattern to the archetype label
3. immediately check chapter distribution and dominant dimensions before making a stronger claim

That keeps the archetype useful as shorthand without letting it harden into over-interpretation.
